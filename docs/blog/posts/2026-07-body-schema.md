# The Body-Schema Paradox

**Question:** Can we understand physics without having a body?

<!-- more -->

**Research Abstract:**
Embodied cognition argues that cognition is shaped by sensorimotor contingencies and body constraints. A “body schema” (functional model of one’s body in action) is not decorative; it can be constitutive of certain concepts.

**Anchor Refs:**
Pfeifer & Bongard, *How the Body Shapes the Way We Think* (2006).

**Additional Notes**
1. World Models (The "Physicalist" Path)
Lead by figures like Yann LeCun, this paradigm argues that AGI must be "grounded" in the physical world.
The Theory: To have common sense, an AI must understand physics (gravity, causality, persistence).
Why it’s AGI-focused: It moves away from language as the primary source of truth. By training on video and sensory data, the goal is a "Foundation Model of Reality" that can plan and navigate the world as a toddler does, rather than just talking about it.

**Story Beat:**
We move the mind into the world and expect understanding to snap into place.
