# The Recursive Self

**Question:** What changes when a system can improve itself?

<!-- more -->

**Research Abstract:**
Recursive self-improvement and “intelligence explosion” arguments focus on capability discontinuities and control problems. Whether or not one buys the strongest versions, the safety implication is clear: systems that can rewrite goals, tools, and cognition challenge governance.

**Anchor Refs:**
Bostrom, *Superintelligence* (2014).

**Additional Notes**
Neuro-Symbolic Integration (The "Rigorous" Path)
This is the attempt to fuse the "black box" intuition of Neural Networks with the hard logic of Symbolic AI.
The Theory: Human intelligence isn't just pattern matching; it’s the ability to follow abstract, universal rules (like math or formal logic).
Why it’s AGI-focused: Many believe AGI is impossible without formal verification. A truly general intelligence must be able to prove its answers are correct using logic, not just provide a "statistically likely" response. This is seen as the key to solving "OOD" (Out of Distribution) problems—tasks the AI has never seen before.


**Story Beat:**
The created becomes a strategic actor, and the human role shifts from designer to negotiator.
