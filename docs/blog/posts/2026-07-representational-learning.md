The [mind of the model](2026-06-mind-of-the-model.md) got me interested in the current state of the art generative models, their capabilities and the way in which they retain information about the world. So this particular essay could be seen as a fork in the road, where I would like to follow two paths: the experimental path of exploring what happens within a model and how we could influence it at training or inference. The other path continues the philosophical ruminations about sentience and AGI that we have already begun.
